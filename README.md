🏪 Wholesale E-Commerce Platform - Addis Ababa
A B2B wholesale marketplace platform designed specifically for Ethiopian businesses in Addis Ababa. Connecting suppliers with local shop owners, businesses, and managers for bulk purchasing of everyday essentials.

🌍 Local Focus
This platform is tailored for Ethiopia (Addis Ababa region) with payment and delivery solutions that work within local constraints, avoiding international payment gateways that don't function in our region.

🚀 Features
📦 Product Categories
Dairy Products: Milk, Yogurt, Cheese

Beverages: Soft drinks, Juices, Water

Snacks: Chips, Biscuits, Cookies

Packaged Fast Foods: Indomie, Pasta, Instant noodles

More coming soon...

🏢 For Business Users
Shop Owners & Managers - Bulk purchasing for retail businesses

Restaurants & Cafes - Regular supply orders

Distributors - Manage multiple business accounts

💼 Core Features
B2B Account Management - Business verification and registration

Bulk Ordering System - Special pricing for volume purchases

Local Payment Methods - Solutions that work in Ethiopia

Inventory Management - Real-time stock tracking

Price Negotiation - Direct communication with suppliers

Delivery Scheduling - Flexible delivery options for Addis Ababa
